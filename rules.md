# Operating Rules

These rules are the default operating system. Change them deliberately during a review, not in the middle of a difficult study session.

1. **Never skip two consecutive days.** A short recovery session counts.
2. **Input → Recall → Output → Feedback.** Complete the loop whenever possible.
3. **Speaking first, grammar second.** Use grammar to improve communication, not postpone it.
4. **Mistakes are assets.** Capture recurring errors and turn them into future practice.
5. **Prefer active recall over rereading.** Try to retrieve before checking the answer.
6. **Study useful language in context.** Record phrases, examples, and situations instead of isolated trivia.
7. **Keep the daily minimum small.** Consistency matters more than heroic sessions.
8. **Review before collecting more.** Reuse existing vocabulary and mistakes before expanding the backlog.
9. **Measure evidence, not mood.** Track what was said, written, understood, corrected, or remembered.
10. **Make every entry easy to find.** Use stable dates, language names, and predictable Markdown.
11. **Protect the long-term system.** Avoid tools, formats, or workflows that create unnecessary maintenance.
