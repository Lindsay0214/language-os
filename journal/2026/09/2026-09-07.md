## Feedback

- Grammar repair: 3/5 correct on the `type of` / `kind of` / `a lot of` targets (#2, #3, #5). Two misses:
  - #1 `full of interested` → `full of interest` — this is the *exact* Day 17 error repeated. `full of` takes a noun, not a participle. 2nd occurrence.
  - #4 `felt really boring` → `felt really bored` — feeling-word rule (Day 15/16 family). The person feels it, so `-ed`. This is the one place the two rules touch: `boring` is wrong here because it describes the wrong thing, not because it isn't a noun.
  - #2 also had a tense slip: original was `is ... I always make` (present habit); changing to `This was` breaks agreement with `always make`. A habit that is still true stays present.
- `full of` sentence: the `full of food` slot itself was grammatically fine (`food` is a noun), so the target rule landed. The surrounding sentence had separate problems: `My sister's company event held on last Saturday` has no finite verb (`held` is a participle) → `My sister's company held an event last Saturday`; `last Saturday` takes no `on`; `after event` → `after the event` (countable singular needs an article); the trailing `which`-clause is the Day 7 two-ideas-in-one-sentence pattern; `mambers` → `members`.
- German: `speren` is an invented verb (same pattern as `swimmist` on Day 16 — an English sound given a German-looking ending). "to speak" is `sprechen`; the language is `Englisch`. Sentence 2 used the English verbs `hike`/`paint` directly (`wandern`/`malen`). The *structure* of both sentences was correct — `Ich kann` + object + infinitive(s) at the end, and `und` joining two infinitives cleanly. The gap is vocabulary, not grammar.
