## Feedback

- `full of` drill: **1/5** on the noun-form target, and the one correct answer (`noise`) needed no transformation because the bracket word was already a noun. The other four:
  - #2 `energation` — invented word; the noun is just `energy` (the bracket gave it).
  - #3 `kind` — kept the adjective; needed `kindness` (warmth toward people).
  - #4 `silent` — adjective; needed `silence`.
  - #5 `confident` — adjective; needed `confidence`.
  - Pattern: the bracket hints `(noise) (energy) (silence) (confidence)` were already nouns — four of five just needed to be copied in unchanged, and an `-ing`/`-ed`/`-t` adjective or an invented word came out instead. This is not a "which noun form" problem; `full of` is triggering the adjective reflex before the noun is considered. The Day 18 cue has not taken hold.
  - New cue: after `full of`, the next word must be one that accepts `the` in front of it (`the noise`, `the kindness` — fine; `the silent`, `the confident` — not English).
- `full of` original sentence: the `full of dust` slot itself was correct (`dust` is a noun). Rest of the sentence: `unfreash` is not a word (→ `stale` / `stuffy`; "fresh" has no `un-` form); the `which`-clause chains dirty AC → stale air → I cleaned it into one sentence (Day 7 / Day 18 pattern); `I clean up yesterday` → `I cleaned it out yesterday` (past tense with `yesterday`, and `clean` needs an object). Polished: "My air conditioner was full of dust, so the air felt stale. I cleaned it out yesterday."
- German: **2/2 correct.** `Ich kann wandern.` (new verb from the allowed list, structure right) and `Ich kann nicht malen.` (negative — `nicht` before the infinitive at the end). Stayed entirely inside the supplied verb list — no invented verbs, which was the specific risk after Day 16 (`swimmist`) and Day 18 (`speren`). First fully clean German output since Day 15.
