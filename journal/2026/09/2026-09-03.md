## Feedback

- English: sentence boundaries were clean and well-separated this time, directly addressing the Day 15 observation about run-on speech. Correction: `really satisfied for me` → `really satisfying for me` — `satisfied` describes a person's feeling, `satisfying` describes what causes that feeling. This is the third occurrence of the same underlying pattern (feeling-word noun/adjective/participle confusion), following `excitement`/`excited` (Day 12) and `frustration`/`frustrating` (Day 15). Worth consolidating into one general rule rather than tracking each word pair separately.
- German: attempted `Ich kann...` (a new, not-yet-taught pattern) with an invented word `swimmist` (English "swim" with a German-sounding ending tacked on). The sentence structure itself (`Ich kann` + verb) was the right instinct; the issue is vocabulary, not grammar — `schwimmen` (to swim) is an unrelated German word, not derivable from English. Correct form: `Ich kann schwimmen.`

## Captures
