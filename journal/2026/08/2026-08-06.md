## Key Mistakes

### English

- `breathe` vs `breath`
- Present tense agreement: `Google takes`, not `Google take`
- Use `plan to + verb`.
- Use `ask someone to do something`.

### German

- Conjugate `haben`: `Ich habe`, not `Ich hast`.
- Use `sein` in `Ich bin klein`.
- Use the correct articles and cases: `eine Schwester und einen Bruder`.

## Next Focus
