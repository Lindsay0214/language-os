## Feedback

- English: successfully connected reason and result in one sentence using `because`, meeting today's target. Corrections: `felt more energy` → `had more energy` (or `felt more energetic`) — `energy` is a noun and cannot follow `felt` directly as a complement; `beacause` → `because` (spelling).
- German: this attempt directly translated English sentence structure (`felt` + comparative + adverb), which does not map onto German grammar. Rather than correcting word-by-word, offered two simpler, already-practiced patterns instead: `Ich bin heute besser` (using `sein` + comparative adjective `besser`) or `Ich bin froh` (using `sein` + a simple A1 adjective). No rewrite required from the learner today — noted as a pattern to watch: relying on already-practiced `sein`/`haben` structures is more reliable than translating English feeling-verbs at this stage. `jezte` (intended `jetzt`) is a third distinct misspelling of the same word across sessions.

## Captures

- Mistakes to add to `mistakes/english.md`: `felt more energy` → `had more energy` / `felt more energetic` (noun vs. adjective after `felt`).
- Mistakes to add to `mistakes/german.md`: direct English-to-German structure translation (`felt + comparative + adverb`) does not work; prefer practiced `sein`/`haben` patterns. `jetzt` misspelled a third way (`jezte`).
- Vocabulary to add to `vocabulary/german.md`: `besser` (better), `froh` (glad/happy) as safer alternatives to translating `felt`.
