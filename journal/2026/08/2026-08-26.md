## Feedback

- Speaking: content is genuine and personally grounded, but the answer stayed at the Example level without a clear Result sentence tying back to specifics (e.g., "consistent small weekend effort builds real progress over time"). Minor corrections: `spend my weekend` → `spend my weekends` (habitual plural), `learning language` → `learning languages`. Noted: the content mentions applying for the A1 test "today," which is inconsistent with the recent decision to postpone the Goethe A1 exam — flagged only as a writing-content note, not a plan change.
- Grammar repair: 2/3 correct. Sentences 1 and 2 correctly used `felt + adjective`. Sentence 3 used the noun `excitement` instead of the adjective `excited` (`She felt a excitement` → `She felt excited`) — the error itself demonstrates understanding of the underlying rule (feeling-verb needs an adjective complement), just a wrong part-of-speech choice. This closely tracks the Day 11 pattern and shows it is now mostly internalized.
- German: both sentences correctly avoided direct English-structure translation and used `haben`. New error identified: `haben` was not conjugated for the `ich` subject — should be `habe`, not the infinitive `haben`. Also: `die Wasser` should have no article (`Wasser` is uncountable — this was correct back on Day 6, so this is a slip, not a new gap); `der Hund` should be `einen Hund` (masculine accusative after `haben`) — this pattern was marked resolved after Day 9, so its reappearance here means it needs continued light monitoring rather than being fully closed.
