# Reusable Story Bank

Store personal experiences that can be reused across IELTS Speaking, English Writing, and beginner German. The goal is to change the language and structure, not invent a new story every time.

## Story Template

- Topic:
- Situation:
- Main action:
- Challenge:
- Result:
- Useful language:
- IELTS questions it can answer:
- German A1 version:

## Freediving and Breathing

- Topic: Learning breathing techniques
- Situation: Breathing matters in freediving and yoga.
- Main action: Learn from online videos, courses, and an instructor.
- Challenge: Breathing does not feel as strong or comfortable as it should.
- Result: Better breathing could improve performance and help maintain calm underwater.
- Useful language: `breathing techniques`, `breathe more deeply`, `improve my performance`, `maintain concentration`
- IELTS questions it can answer: a skill to learn; a sport; a health habit; a difficult goal
- German A1 version: `Ich lerne Atemübungen. Ich mache Yoga. Ich möchte besser atmen.`

## Childhood Asthma

- Topic: A health experience that shaped a goal
- Situation: The learner had asthma as a child.
- Main action: Pay more attention to breathing and physical activities.
- Challenge: Some activities require controlled breathing.
- Result: The experience created a long-term interest in breathing and health.
- Useful language: `when I was a child`, `pay attention to`, `long-term interest`
- IELTS questions it can answer: a childhood experience; a health decision; a personal challenge
- German A1 version: `Als Kind hatte ich Asthma.`

## Working from Home

- Topic: Productivity and work environment
- Situation: The learner works from home.
- Main action: Join online meetings and manage work independently.
- Challenge: It is easier to become comfortable or lose focus at home.
- Result: A structured routine can improve concentration.
- Useful language: `work from home`, `lose focus`, `maintain concentration`, `structured routine`
- IELTS questions it can answer: a workplace; productivity; technology; a daily routine
- German A1 version: `Ich arbeite von zu Hause. Ich habe Online-Meetings.`

## Front-End Engineer

- Topic: Work and professional identity
- Situation: The learner works as a front-end engineer.
- Main action: Build interfaces and solve practical problems.
- Challenge: Technical work requires concentration and continuous learning.
- Result: The job develops problem-solving skills.
- Useful language: `solve practical problems`, `continuous learning`, `problem-solving skills`
- IELTS questions it can answer: a job; a useful skill; technology; future plans
- German A1 version: `Ich bin Frontend-Entwickler. Ich arbeite mit Computern.`

## Learning German

- Topic: Starting a new language
- Situation: The learner recently started German and is below A1.
- Main action: Practice small sentences every day.
- Challenge: New word order and articles feel unfamiliar.
- Result: Small daily practice makes the language easier to resume.
- Useful language: `start from the basics`, `practice every day`, `make gradual progress`
- IELTS questions it can answer: a new skill; a difficult subject; a goal
- German A1 version: `Ich lerne Deutsch. Deutsch ist neu für mich. Ich übe jeden Tag.`

## Moving Abroad

- Topic: A future plan or major life change
- Situation: The learner is interested in living abroad.
- Main action: Improve English and German and prepare practical skills.
- Challenge: Moving requires communication, planning, and adaptation.
- Result: Language learning supports long-term independence.
- Useful language: `adapt to a new environment`, `long-term independence`, `prepare for a major change`
- IELTS questions it can answer: a future plan; a place to live; a major change
- German A1 version: `Ich möchte im Ausland leben. Ich lerne Sprachen.`
