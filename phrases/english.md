## Day 3

### financial literacy

The ability to understand and manage money.

Example: Schools should teach financial literacy as well as academic subjects.

### first aid

Basic medical help given during an emergency.

Example: Learning first aid could help students save a life.

### breathing techniques

Methods for controlling or improving breathing.

Example: I want to learn breathing techniques for freediving and yoga.

### improve performance

Become better at doing an activity.

Example: Better breathing can improve my performance underwater.

### maintain concentration

Continue focusing without losing attention.

Example: Short breaks can help people maintain concentration.

### improve my performance

Use this phrase for a personal goal instead of the unnatural `do more extreme sports deep`.

Example: I want to improve my performance in freediving.

### maintain financial stability

Keep money and spending in a stable condition.

Example: Good financial planning helps people maintain financial stability.

### place more emphasis on

Give something greater importance.

Example: Schools should place more emphasis on practical skills.

### as a result

Introduce a consequence in formal writing.

Example: People who understand budgeting are less likely to overspend. As a result, they can maintain financial stability.

### for these reasons

Introduce a conclusion based on previous points.

Example: For these reasons, practical skills should receive more attention in schools.
